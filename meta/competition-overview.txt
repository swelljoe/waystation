Overview

Billions of people spend their lives in digital spaces - games, social feeds, creator studios, fitness apps, developer tools, wearables - where Scripture has never been present in any meaningful way. Not because it doesn't belong there. Because no one has built the bridge yet.

This challenge invites you to change that.

Using the YouVersion Platform API and the Gloo AI Studio API, your task is to build an experience that integrates Scripture natively into an emerging digital context. Not as an afterthought or a pop-up - but as something that feels like it was designed for that environment from the start.

Description

What you'll build Your submission should demonstrate Scripture meaningfully integrated into one of the following frontiers or one you identify yourself:

    Gaming - narrative resonance woven into the worlds players already inhabit
    Creator tools - meaning present where creators shape culture
    Wearables - the right word at the right physiological moment
    Developer IDEs - presence in the margins where builders think
    Fitness apps - encouragement tied to effort
    Social platforms - Scripture as conversation, not broadcast

These are invitations, not requirements. If you see a gap we haven't named, build there.

Required APIs Both APIs are required and provided free to all registered participants:

YouVersion Platform API - Scripture in 2,000+ languages, reading plans, verse of the day, user highlights, and community engagement data Gloo AI Studio API - Faith-tuned AI inference, content generation, personalization, and safety infrastructure built for ministry contexts

The goal is not another Bible app. The goal is Scripture present where people already are.

1. Kaggle Writeup

The Kaggle Writeup serves as your project report. This should include a title, subtitle, and a detailed analysis of your submission.

Your Writeup should not exceed 500 words. Submissions over this limit may be subject to penalty.

The below assets must be attached to the Writeup to be eligible.
a. Media Gallery

This is where you should attach any images and/or videos associated with your submission. A cover image is required to submit your Writeup.
b. Public Notebook

Your code should be submitted as a public notebook in the Project Files field. Your notebook should be publicly accessible and not require a login or paywall. If you use a private Kaggle Notebook, it will automatically be made public after the deadline.
c. Video

Attach your video to the Media Gallery. Videos should be 3 minutes or less, and should be published to Youtube.
d. Public Project Link

A URL to your working product or interactive demo. This allows judges to experience your project firsthand, if applicable. It should be publicly accessible and not require a login or paywall. If a live demo is not feasible, a link to your public code repository (e.g., GitHub) is required, including detailed setup instructions.

Evaluation

Using the YouVersion Platform API and the Gloo AI Studio API, your job is to integrate Scripture natively into an emerging digital context - not as a pop-up, not as an afterthought, but as something that feels like it was always meant to be there.

Your submission will be judged primarily on your video demo. This is your moment to show something genuinely exciting that could reach millions of people. Tell a story. Show the problem. Show how your solution changes what's possible for someone encountering Scripture in a space they already love.

That said, all projects must be backed by real, working technology. Your writeup and code repo give judges the confidence that what they saw in the video is actually built, not just mocked up. Code will be reviewed.

What to Submit

At minimum, it should include:

🎬 The Video Demo (3 minutes or less - this is the most important part) Make it dynamic. Make it compelling. Tell us the story of the person whose life looks different because Scripture showed up somewhere it never had before. Post it publicly on YouTube where judges can access without logging in. Drop the direct link in your writeup.

📄 The Technical Writeup (Your proof of work) This is your blog-post or paper-style report. Walk us through your architecture, explain how you used the YouVersion and Gloo APIs, share the challenges you hit and how you worked through them, and make the case for why your technical choices were the right ones. Its job is simple: prove to the judges that your demo is backed by real engineering.

💻 The Code Repository (Non-negotiable) Link to a public repo on GitHub, Kaggle Notebook, wherever you work. Your code should be well-documented and clearly show your implementation. This is how we verify the authenticity of your project. No private repos.

How You'll Be Judged

Your video is the primary lens. Your writeup and code provide the technical depth and verification behind it.

Here's how points break down:

Impact & Vision — 40 points How clearly and compellingly does your project address a real problem? We're looking for solutions that feel genuinely meaningful, where Scripture integration isn't just clever, it's transformative for the person using it. Is the vision inspiring? Could this change something at scale?

Video Pitch & Storytelling — 30 points Is the video exciting? Does it tell a story that sticks with you? Does it demonstrate the product in action and make the user experience feel real and compelling? Could this go viral? This is where you get to show the soul of your project. Don't hold back.

Technical Depth & Execution — 30 points As verified by your code and writeup: How innovative is your use of the YouVersion and Gloo APIs? Is the technology genuinely functional and well-engineered and not just faked for the demo? We're looking for builders who pushed the tools to do something new.
